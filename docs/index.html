The save editor for RPG Maker and Ren'Py games

Change gold, items, stats, switches and variables in games made with RPG Maker MV, MZ, VX Ace, VX, XP and Ren'Py. Free, open source, and it runs on your PC: nothing is uploaded.

Download for Windows View on GitHub

For Windows 10 and 11 (64-bit). Updates itself when a new version is released.

  • RPG Maker MV

  • RPG Maker VX
  • RPG Maker XP
  • Ren'Py 7 and 8

Everything in your save, by name

Item, switch and variable names come from the game's own data, so you see "Potion" and "Bridge repaired" instead of ID numbers.

  • Party and gold

    Gold, HP, MP, TP, level, EXP and bonus stats of every character.

  • Items, weapons, armors

    Set how many of each you own, with names and descriptions from the game.

  • Switches and variables

    Turn story switches on or off and change counters, quest steps and flags.

  • Ren'Py variables

    Money, affection points, flags and names in visual novels, kept in their original type.

  • Search every value

    The Advanced tab shows the whole save as a tree, with text, number and path search.

  • Find unknown values

    Compare two saves of the same game to find the values that changed, went up or went down.

  • Safe for your saves

    What you don't edit is kept as is, and unsaved changes are never lost by accident.

  • Encrypted games too

    Reads names from Game.rgssad, Game.rgss2a and Game.rgss3a archives.

  • Made to be pleasant

    Light and dark themes, drag and drop, keyboard shortcuts and one-click updates.

Where are the save files?

Open the slot you saved in: file3 or Save03 is the third slot. global and persistent files hold settings, not your progress.

Ren'Py 7 and 8game/saves/ and %APPDATA%\RenPy\<game>1-1-LT1.save

Edit a save in four steps

  1. Back it up

    Copy your save file somewhere safe first.

  2. Open it

    Press Ctrl+O or drag the save onto the window.

  3. Change values

    Edited values are highlighted, and each one can be undone.

  4. Save and play

    Press Ctrl+S, then load the save in the game.

Ren'Py notes and the full guide are in the README on GitHub.

Privacy policy

RPG Save Editor works offline and does not collect or send any information about you or your saves.

The only connection it makes on its own is the update check: at startup and when you click Check for Updates, it downloads update information from GitHub Pages and, if you choose to update, the installer from GitHub. You can turn the automatic check off in About. Links open in your browser only when you click them.

To uninstall, open Settings → Apps → Installed apps, find rpgsaveeditor and click Uninstall. Your saves are not touched.
